PepTrack Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

In short: PepTrack has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data stays on your device and in places you choose. We (the developer) never receive it.

What PepTrack stores

The information you enter (peptides and medications, doses, schedules, injection sites, vial and reconstitution details, titration plans, side effects, notes and settings) is stored on your device. It never goes to the developer.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data syncs through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), protected by your Apple Account. The developer can't access it. Sync is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time.

Backups and exports

Backups and exports are files you create and control (for example in Files or iCloud Drive). Any API key you add for Ask AI is stored only in this device's Keychain and is never included in backups, exports or iCloud sync.

Apple Health

If you allow it, PepTrack reads selected Health data (such as weight, blood pressure and resting heart rate) to show it next to your dose history. PepTrack never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or sold, and the app never sends it anywhere. The one exception is if you choose to include a summary in an Ask AI request (see below).

Ask AI (optional)

Ask AI is off by default. If you turn it on, you choose an AI provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or a server you specify) and enter your own API key. Data is sent only when you tap Send (or Weekly Summary / Test Connection), and only to the provider you chose. You see exactly what will be shared first, and you can leave out Health data and side effects. Data goes directly from your device to that provider; the developer never sees it. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what you send.

Notifications

Dose and vial reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no third-party code.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

PepTrack is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove its on-device data. If you used iCloud sync, you can remove the synced data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Not medical advice

PepTrack is a tracking and logging tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not recommend doses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
